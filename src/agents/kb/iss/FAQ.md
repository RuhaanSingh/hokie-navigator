## Top 10 Most Frequently Asked Questions

#### These are some of the most frequently asked questions Cranwell International Center receives For further questions, please email us at international@vt.edu.

## Where and when can I get a travel signature?

Please visit the [IntlHokies Portal](https://international.vt.edu/intlhokies.html) to submit the e-form or visit us during mid-semester travel signature events.

## When, how, and why should I get my Social Security number?

Please see our [Social Security instructions](https://international.vt.edu/employment/ssn.html). If you have additional questions, please visit our office during walk–in hours or email [international@vt.edu](mailto:international@vt.edu).

## How do I start CPT?

Do you need CEIP or Co-Op approval for CPT? Start that process first! You must complete either CEIP undergraduate course or the graduate Co-Op process before you submit your CPT request. Please see our [CPT basics page](https://international.vt.edu/employment/cpt.html).

- Undergraduates, please visit the [CEIP page on the Career and Professional Development website](https://career.vt.edu/resources/ceip-students/) to start this process.

- Graduate students, please visit the [Co-op page on the Graduate School website](https://graduateschool.vt.edu/student-services/GradCoop.html).

## How do I start OPT and what documents do I need?

- Please see our [OPT basics page](https://international.vt.edu/employment/opt.html).

- You must complete the [OPT Self Enrollment Course](https://canvas.vt.edu/enroll/XA8DPX) before being approved for OPT.

## How do I get a STEM OPT extension?

Please see our [OPT Extension page](https://international.vt.edu/employment/24-month_STEM_OPT_Extension.html).

## When and how do I change to H-1B?

Please read this [FAQ from Global Strategic Services](https://gss.vt.edu/foreignnational/H-1B/FAQ.html).

## As an international parent, can I ask questions about my child’s status or academics?

Students are protected under FERPA laws. Please have your child contact and speak with us directly at [international@vt.edu](mailto:international@vt.edu). For more information about FERPA, please visit [Virginia Tech's FERPA page on the Registrar's website](https://www.registrar.vt.edu/FERPA.html).

## When is my Orientation?

International students will have a couple of orientations:

- International Student Orientation is broken into undergraduate and graduate students. International orientation dates, which are usually earlier than other orientations, will be listed on the Cranwell New Student page, in the Canvas Course of which all incoming international students are automatically enrolled, and in emails in the months leading up to your arrival.

- Undergraduates: you also have Undergraduate Orientation to attend through New Student and Family Programs.

- Please make sure you complete the mandatory [virtual orientation](https://orientation.vt.edu/).

- Please read through [You're In...Now What](https://nowwhat.vt.edu/).

- Read the Weeks of Welcome schedule on the [Hokies on Track App](https://students.vt.edu/transition/ontrack.html).

- Graduate Students: you also have The Graduate School's Orientation and your department's orientation to attend as well.

- The Graduate School will post the schedule on their [orientation website](https://graduateschool.vt.edu/admissions/orientation.html).

- Please be in contact with your department about their orientation schedule. We ask that you prioritize those.

## How do I find out about my housing?

- Athletes and cadets, you have your own move-in schedule. Please defer to those lines of communication.

- International Undergraduate Student Move-In is a few days before the undergraduate domestic student population. Cranwell and Housing will both communicate this exact date in the months leading up to orientation through email, the Canvas Course, and the StarRez Portal. You can also check the orientation schedule on our [New Student Page](https://international.vt.edu/new_students/undergrads.html)

- International Graduate students, please balance your arrival date with your International Graduate Orientation date, Graduate Orientation date, and start of your lease or housing arrangement.

- Early move-in for undergraduates: Housing offers a limited number of early move-in slots. However, there are limitations to how far out you are allowed to move-in. Housing will release the exact dates and registration option in the two months leading up to the domestic move-in dates. You can check these dates and register through the StarRez Portal. Please note: There is an additional fee based on your housing arrangement for an early move-in registration.

- Undergraduates: For all information related to housing, visit the [Star Rez Portal](https://vt.starrezhousing.com/StarRezPortalX/Login). For questions, email Housing at [Housing@vt.edu](mailto:Housing@vt.edu).

- Graduate Students: If you need help securing housing, please use the [Off-Campus Housing website](https://offcampus.vt.edu/) as a resource.

## How do I obtain proof of enrollment and/or degree completion?

For any questions about registration or enrollment, please visit the [Academic Records page on the Registrar's Office website.](https://www.registrar.vt.edu/academic-records/Certifications-Verifications.html)
