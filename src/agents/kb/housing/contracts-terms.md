## Terms and Conditions of Your Housing Contract

## Housing/Dining Contract Terms and Conditions

Students should familiarize themselves with the terms and conditions of their Housing/Dining Contract. Additional information on housing policies such as visitation, approved appliances to bring with you, and others can be found in the [Virginia Tech Student Code of Conduct](https://codeofconduct.vt.edu/).

Select the terms and conditions that apply to your student status. All forms are in Adobe PDF format. If you have trouble opening the PDF, contact Housing Services at [housing@vt.edu](mailto:housing@vt.edu) or call 540-231-6205 and the contract will be mailed to you.
