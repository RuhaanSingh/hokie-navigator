## Residential Experience

### Residential Experience

**When you're here, you're home!** Your introduction to Hokie Nation starts with your residential experience — from Housing Services to Living-Learning Programs to Residential Well-being — we're here to help you build community, make connections, and truly ExperienceVT.

[Important Dates & Events](https://housing.vt.edu/contracts/events.html)

## Housing Services

With 10,500 on-campus student residents, Housing Services is your go-to resource for all your day-to-day needs — whether it's signing your housing contract, selecting your residence hall, understanding your housing bill, navigating the housing portal, or putting in a work order, we're here to support you!

[Housing Services](https://housing.vt.edu/experience.html)

## Living-Learning Programs

More than 20 Living-Learning Programs (LLPs) create spaces for students to connect and build meaningful communities! By choosing an LLP, you will make the most of your Virginia Tech experience in and outside the classroom by being with peers and world-class faculty who share common interests or experiences.

[Living-Learning Programs](https://llp.vt.edu/)

## Residential Well-being

As a Hokie, you are encouraged and empowered in all aspects of your well-being! Our innovative approach to residential living and learning promotes individual care, student success, and community support with embedded counselors and coordinators that focus on well-being, ExperienceVT, and Inclusion & Belonging.

[Residential Well-being](https://rwb.vt.edu/)
