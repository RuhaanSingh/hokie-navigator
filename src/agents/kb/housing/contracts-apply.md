## Apply for Housing

#### Be sure to check out our Living-Learning Programs!

Many returning students are offered on-campus housing. Housing remaining after the freshman class size is predicted is allocated to returning students through a few different processes, depending on their current status. Most students return to campus housing through the Living Learning Returner application process. All students are encouraged to apply for on-campus housing.

In the menu, students can find the application process that pertains to their level in the university.
