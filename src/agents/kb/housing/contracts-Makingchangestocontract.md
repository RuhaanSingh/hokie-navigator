## Changing or Terminating Your Housing Contract

We understand that living on campus can be difficult while balancing your studies and extra curriculars. If you feel you need to make changes to your housing, talk with your Student Leader first. The resources here can help you navigate the process. Contact your SL or our office with further questions.
