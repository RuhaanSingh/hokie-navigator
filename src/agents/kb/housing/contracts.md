## Your Housing Contract

Virginia Tech boasts world-class award-winning dining centers and provides students with a wide range of on-campus housing options. All first-year students are required to live on campus, are guaranteed housing, and must have a dining plan. However, students must complete their housing/dining contract to be assigned a room. Returning undergraduate students, transfer students, and graduate students are not guaranteed on-campus housing and must apply.

In this section, students can find their housing application process, information about various housing types, terms and conditions, room and board rates, and more.
