## Contact Us | Office of University Scholarships and Financial Aid

We're here to help you navigate your financial aid journey at Virginia Tech.

Whether you have questions about applying for aid, understanding your award, or maintaining eligibility, the Office of University Scholarships and Financial Aid (USFA) is ready to assist you.
