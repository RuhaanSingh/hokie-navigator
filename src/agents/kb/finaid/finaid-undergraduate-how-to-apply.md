## How to Apply for Aid

## Applying for and Receiving Aid

### The Free Application for Student Aid (FAFSA)

File your FAFSA each year to be reviewed for federal and Virginia state financial aid programs and for institutional scholarship and grant programs. For maximum scholarship and financial aid consideration file your FAFSA and the General Scholarship Application by January 22.

Federal Student Aid, the office of the US Department of Education that manages the FAFSA, notified colleges and universities in early 2023 that the 2024-2025 FAFSA will not be available by it's regular open date of October 1. The 2024-2025 FAFSA will now be available on December 31.

### The Virginia Alternative State Aid (VASA) application

Review this option if you are a Virginia student otherwise ineligible to file the FAFSA. Virginia Tech's priority filing deadline for state aid programs is March 1.

### Transfer Students

Visit our Transfer Students Guide to find out more about transferring to Virginia Tech. You can also visit our Academic Scholarships.

### Verification

The U.S. Department of Education randomly selects a percentage of FAFSAs each year to complete the verification process. This process services as a double-check of your FAFSA information. If selected, we’ll contact you via your VT email address and [Financial Aid Portal](https://selfservice.banner.vt.edu/StudentSelfService/ssb/financialAid) with further details on how to complete this. Don’t worry, we’re here to help you every step of the way! For more information, click the Verification heading above.

### Important Financial Aid/Scholarship Dates

- **Late August** - VT Scholarship Central opens for prospective and current students
- **October 1** - The Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) opens. (Please note, Federal Student Aid notified colleges and universities that the 2024-2025 FAFSA will not open on October 1, 2023. We will update this date as soon as we know when the 2024-2025 FAFSA will be available.)
- **January 22** - deadline to complete the FAFSA and General Scholarship Application for maxmimum scholarship and financial aid consideration
- **Late February** - Early Action admissions notifications sent including financial aid/scholarship notifications
- **March 1** - priority deadline for Virginia State Financial Aid Programs and institutional aid
- **Early March** - Regular Decision admissions notifications sent including financial aid/ scholarship notifications
- **Early June** - continuing student financial aid/scholarship notifications sent
- **Mid - July** - fall semester bills available
- **August 10** - fall semester bills due
