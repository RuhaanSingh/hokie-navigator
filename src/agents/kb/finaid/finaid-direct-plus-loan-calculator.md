## Direct PLUS Loan Calculator

## Why Do You Need a PLUS Loan Calculator?

When applying for a Federal PLUS Loan, it's important to account for the origination fee, which reduces the actual amount paid to a student’s billing statement. This means the amount you apply for must be slightly higher than the amount needed to ensure there is no balance due after your PLUS loan is processed.

## How to Use Our PLUS Loan Calculator

To simplify the process, our PLUS Loan Calculator provides **two ways** to determine the correct amount to request on your Parent PLUS Loan application.

#### Option 1: If You Already Know the Amount Needed

- Enter the amount you need in the **“Amount Needed”** field.
- The calculator will automatically display the **“PLUS Gross Amount”**—this is the total amount you should request on your PLUS Loan application, which includes the origination fee.

#### Option 2: If You Need to Determine the Amount Needed

- Enter your **Tuition and Fees** and **Housing and Food** costs.
- Enter any **Scholarships and Grants** you have received.
- Enter any **Accepted Student Loans** that are already covering a portion of the costs.
- The calculator will show the remaining expenses you need to cover as well as the **PLUS Gross Amount, **accounting for the origination fee. The PLUS Gross Amount is the amount you should request on your PLUS Loan Application.
