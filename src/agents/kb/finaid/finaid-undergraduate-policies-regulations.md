## Forms, Policies & Regulations

### Family Educational Rights & Privacy Act, FERPA

Virginia Tech complies with [FERPA](https://www2.ed.gov/policy/gen/guid/fpco/ferpa/index.html) and will not disclose personal information or educational records unless a FERPA passcode is provided. FERPA can be set up by the student in Hokie SPA.

### Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP)

This policy requires students to meet federal guidelines in order to receive financial aid.
