## Withdrawing or resigning and your aid
Source: https://finaid.vt.edu/undergraduate/policies-regulations/enrollment-withdrawal.html

Withdrawing or resigning after the semester starts may reduce financial aid and leave a balance owed to the Bursar; the withdrawal or resignation date drives the calculation. Students should talk with a financial aid counselor and the Bursar before resigning.

## Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP)
Source: https://finaid.vt.edu/undergraduate/policies-regulations/satisfactory-academic-progress-sap.html

Students must meet Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP) standards (GPA, pace of progress, maximum timeframe) to keep federal, state, and institutional aid; failing SAP suspends aid eligibility. Students can appeal, with a limit of two SAP appeals during their time at VT. (Appeals: https://finaid.vt.edu/appeals/satisfactory-academic-progress.html)

## Course withdrawal (W grade)
Source: https://eng.vt.edu/undergraduate/resources-support/how-to/course-withdraw.html

Course withdrawal (W grade) is allowed after the drop deadline for up to three courses in an undergraduate career; a W does not count in GPA. Students using financial aid should consult the financial aid office before withdrawing.
