#### Apply and Receive Aid

Virginia Tech uses the FAFSA and our General Application in Scholarship Central to determine eligibility for many scholarships including general scholarships from our office, grants, work study, and federal loans.

#### Prospective Hokie Webpage

Planning for college starts with understanding your financial aid options. Our Prospective Undergraduate Hokies webpage brings together the information and resources you need to navigate financial aid at Virginia Tech, including important dates, applying for aid, scholarships, grants, loans, costs, and more.

#### Information About Your Financial Offer

View your financial aid offer here once your package is available. Incoming students offered admission will receive their financial aid offer with their offer of admission. Returing Hokies - we will notify you of your financial aid for next year in early June.

#### VT Scholarships

Virginia Tech awards scholarships through the Office of University Scholarships and Financial Aid as well as colleges and departments. For maximum financial aid and scholarship consideration, complete your FAFSA and General Scholarship Application by January 22nd each year.

#### Money Matters Informational Sessions

Money Matters registration will open agin in October. These sessions are for incoming students and their families.

#### Hokie Wellness

Hokies can check out Hokie Wellness Financial Services for everything you always wanted to know about personal finance. Schedule a one-on-one appointment with a financial coach or attend a financial wellness workshop.
