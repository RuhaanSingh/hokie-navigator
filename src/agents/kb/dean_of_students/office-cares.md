## CARES (Campus Advocacy, Resources, and Education for Survivors) Program

### CARES (Campus Advocacy, Resources, and Education for Survivors) Program

*****Confidential Campus Resource

#### CARES is a confidential resource that provides support services to students, faculty and staff who have been impacted by experiences such as sexual assault, domestic violence, dating violence, stalking/harassment and many other forms of violence. The CARES Program is a part of the Dean of Students Office.
