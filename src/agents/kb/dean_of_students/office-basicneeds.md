## Basic Needs and Services

#### The Dean of Students' Office is here to support students who are struggling meeting their basic needs while also being a student here at Virginia Tech. If you need additional support with food, housing, finances, technology, mental health resources, childcare, and/or transportation - we would like to connect with you as you navigate options and resources.

#### This includes (but is not limited to): the VT Emergency Grant, the Market Enrollment Program, and the Laptop Loaner Program. Please fill out the form below and then you will be prompted to schedule a meeting with a member of our team.

[Care and Support Meeting Form](https://cm.maxient.com/reportingform.php?VirginiaTech&layout_id=6)
