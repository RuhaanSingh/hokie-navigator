## Student Emergency Fund

Through generous donations provided by many caring Hokies, the Dean of Students Office has been entrusted to manage a fund for students who find themselves in immediate financial need, due to no fault of their own. This means, any student currently enrolled at Virginia Tech can apply for a one-time grant to help during emergent circumstances.

If there are funds available through Financial Aid such as subsidized / unsubsidized loans or a Parent Plus loan, we will encourage these options be used. These funds are limited, so those experiencing the possibility of eviction or food insecurity will be given priority.
