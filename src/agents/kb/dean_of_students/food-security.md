## Food Security

Photo credit: Darren Van Dyke

#### Nearly one in six Virginia Tech students is impacted by food insecurity. The Dean of Students office supports student success with resources and information to increase food access.

## Virginia Tech Food Access Programs

- [The Market of Virginia Tech](https://foodaccess.vt.edu/) is a university program available to all Virginia Tech students in need of food support. The Market is located just off campus and offers drop-in hours each week, students only need to bring a Hokie Passport to use The Market. Visit their website to learn more about each of their programs and view upcoming hours.

## Community Food Access Programs

- [209 Marketplace](https://www.209marketplace.com/) is a student-ran food pantry located just off campus. This program is open to any Virginia Tech student but does require a form for first-time customers. Visit their website to see up-to-date hours and location information.

- [The NRV Food Assistance Directory](https://cfnrv.org/thrive-food-access-network/) is an up-to-date resource that lists all food assistance resources available in the New River Valley.
