## Paying in Monthly Installments

## What is the Budget Tuition Plan?

Pay part or all of your tuition, fees, dining and housing in monthly installments during the semester.

- Payable through U.S. or domestic bank draft only, no credit cards

- $450 minimum balance due to enroll

- $45 application fee per semester (non-refundable)

- Automatically adjusts for credits and charges placed on account (email [bursar@vt.edu](mailto:bursar@vt.edu) for application of debit to time sensitive charges such as parking tickets)

- Can be combined with financial aid, loans or scholarships

- Receive email notice 10 days before with amount to be withdrawn

- No late fees or penalties

[Enroll in Budget Tuition Plan on Transact Payments](https://commerce.cashnet.com/cashneti/static/epayment/vtpay/login)

## Information on Budget Tuition Plan Enrollment

## Enrollment Period & Withdrawal Dates by Semester

### Summer 2026

The summer Budget Tuition Plan opens in early May. The summer payment plan offers 3 -monthly installments due on

- May 15, 2026

- June 15, 2026

- July 15, 2026

### Fall 2026

The fall Budget Tuition Plan opens in mid-July. The fall payment plan offers 4-monthly installments due on

- August 21, 2026

- September 18, 2026

- October 16, 2026

- November 18, 2026

### Spring 2027

The spring Budget Tuition Plan opens in mid-December. The spring payment plan offers 4 -monthly installments due on

- January 15, 2027

- February 19, 2027

- March 19, 2027

- April 16, 2027

**NOTE:** Outstanding balances not satisfied by the payment deadline date will be subject to further penalties such as a late payment fee and account hold. Failure to make financial arrangements by the 10th day of classes may result in dismissal.

*Please refer to your plan enrollment document.
