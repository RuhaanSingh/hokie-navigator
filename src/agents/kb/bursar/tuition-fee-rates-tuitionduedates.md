## Tuition & Fee Due Dates

Due Dates & Late Fees

The Bursar's Office generates billing statements at least monthly for new account activity with full payment due by the statement posted due date. If the account is not paid by the billing due date, a [late-payment fee and hold](https://bursar.vt.edu/tuition-fee-rates/tuitionduedates/latefees.html) may apply. Pay online through the [Hokie SPA](http://www.hokiespa.vt.edu/) or the [Authorized Payer System](https://commerce.cashnet.com/cashneti/static/epayment/vtpay/login).
