## Grade Entry Availability and Deadlines

**Grade Entry at Virginia Tech

**Virginia Tech uses two grade reporting periods — **Midterm** and **Final Grade Entry** — to record student performance each term. **Midterm grade entry**, available only during the **fall semester**, allows instructors to provide progress updates for first semester undergraduate students; these grades are for feedback purposes only and do not appear on the official transcript or affect GPA. **Final grade entry** records the official course grades that determine academic standing, graduation eligibility, and GPA.
